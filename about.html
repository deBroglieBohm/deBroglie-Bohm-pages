About the author

Chris Dewdney was a student in David Bohm's group in the 1970s and later worked with J-P Vigier at Louis de Broglie's institute in Paris. In 1986 he joined Portsmouth University as a Reader and was later Head of Physics. He is now an academic visitor.

His work has focused on the de Broglie-Bohm theory of quantum mechanics, quantum trajectories, measurement, nonlocality and visual simulations of quantum phenomena.

About the ebook

Seeing Through the Quantum Fog is written for readers who are interested in quantum mechanics but do not want to begin with formal mathematics. It uses simulations and visual explanations to show how quantum phenomena can be pictured in the de Broglie-Bohm theory.

Selected topics

  • de Broglie-Bohm theory
  • Quantum trajectories
  • Interference and tunnelling
  • Delayed choice and interferometry
  • Spin measurement
  • Configuration space and nonlocality
