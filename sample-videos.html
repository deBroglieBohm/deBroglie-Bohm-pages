Sample videos

These short samples show the style of the ebook: simulations first, followed by direct explanation. Replace the placeholder video filenames with your own exported MP4 files.

Quantum tunnelling

A wave packet approaches a barrier. Part of the wave is reflected and part is transmitted, while the particle follows one definite trajectory.

The two-slit experiment

The wave passes through both slits and forms an interference pattern. The particle passes through one slit, but its motion is guided by the whole wave.

Delayed choice

A delayed choice changes the experimental arrangement after the particle has entered the apparatus. In the de Broglie-Bohm account, the behaviour follows from the actual wave field present in the final arrangement.
