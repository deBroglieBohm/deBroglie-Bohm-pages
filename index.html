Seeing Through the Quantum Fog

A visual journey through quantum mechanics.

An ebook for curious non-specialists, using video simulations to show how quantum phenomena come about and how they may be explained according to the de Broglie-Bohm theory.

What the book shows

Seeing Through the Quantum Fog illustrates interference, tunnelling, delayed choice, spin measurement, quantisation and other foundational quantum phenomena through the motion of waves and particles.

The aim is not to replace the mathematical theory, but to make visible how individual quantum events can occur and how they give rise to the observed probabilities.

Topics included

  • Quantum tunnelling
  • The two-slit experiment
  • Wheeler's delayed choice experiment
  • Mach-Zehnder interferometry
  • Spin measurement
  • Quantum trajectories
  • Quantisation of energy

Part 1

Part 1 deals with single-particle phenomena and shows how waves, particles and trajectories can be used to visualise otherwise puzzling quantum effects.

Part 2

Part 2 will extend the visual approach to two-particle effects, configuration space, indistinguishability, entanglement and quantum nonlocality.
